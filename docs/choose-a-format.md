# Choose the structure that helps a reader understand your experience

| Format | Lead with | Employment record | Suggested starting point |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chronological | Summary, skills, then recent jobs | Detailed employer/title/date entries | Clear progression in a familiar field |
| Hybrid | Selected capabilities and accomplishments | Employers, titles, dates, and remaining detail | Career changes or unfamiliar military/internal titles |
| Functional | Grouped capabilities with most bullets | Short employer/title/date history | Compare with a coach when experience is varied |
| Employer-light | Accurate primary responsibility headings | Actual title, dates, employer on a secondary line | Reduce brand emphasis while keeping context |

These are content structures with the same single-column typography. They are not guaranteed hiring outcomes. Follow the application's instructions when choosing fields, length, and file format.

CareerOneStop recognizes chronological, functional, and combination formats. It warns that a functional resume's limited work history may be misunderstood or rejected by an ATS, and recommends including employer and dates in work history. A hybrid is a useful starting point for leading with transferable skills while retaining that context. Sources: [format guidance](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/formatting.aspx), [work-experience guidance](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/JobSearch/Resumes/ResumeGuide/work-experience.aspx).

## Employer names: less prominent versus omitted

Employer-light normally prints a responsibility heading, actual title, dates, and a smaller employer line. A functional heading must not imply a title you did not hold.

For comparison or a deliberate networking draft:

```text
python resume.py build examples/demo-person --variant employer-light --anonymous
```

This replaces employer names with organization descriptors and explicitly says `name omitted`. It does not anonymize the candidate or other details. Output gets a separate `employer-light-anonymous` directory, so it cannot overwrite the ordinary version. Employer omission reduces context and may hinder application parsing. Provide complete history when requested, and do not infer that omission is required by every skills-first strategy.

Keep accomplishments attributed to the right role. Hybrid and functional examples retain employer labels on grouped achievements so readers can connect results to the short chronology.

These templates target ordinary private-sector applications. Federal resumes, academic CVs, and regulated applications may need different fields or formats; verify their current requirements separately.
